# Requirements: Personalization

## Functional Requirements (RF)

### Theming
- **RF-THEME-001**: The system shall support Light and Dark modes.
- **RF-THEME-002**: The system shall integrate `dynamic_color` to extract colors from the OS.
- **RF-THEME-003**: The system shall allow the user to toggle between Light, Dark, and System default.
- **RF-THEME-004**: Theme preferences shall be persisted locally.

## Non-Functional Requirements (NRF)
- **NFR-THEME-001**: Theme switching should happen instantly without app restart.
- **NFR-THEME-002**: Contrast ratios must meet accessibility standards (WCAG AA).
